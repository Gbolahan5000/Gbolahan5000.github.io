04 — Projects

MEP Project Cost Analysis

🏗️
Simulated case study · fictional contractor · Excel

Where is material and procurement spend running above plan, and is the overrun concentrated or spread across the portfolio? I answered that for a fictional Nigerian MEP contractor: 80 concurrent projects, 688 work orders, 1,350 purchase orders and ₦21.4bn of PO value.

  • Built the workbook in Power Query and PivotTables over 11 source tables.
  • Added a purchase-order variance column and a portfolio KPI sheet with budget vs actual material cost.
  • Ranked the five worst projects by overrun and drilled into the top one by discipline and work category.
  • Reconciled totals, checked duplicate keys and orphans, and wrote up the data's limitations.
