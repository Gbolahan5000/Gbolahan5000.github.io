Business Data Checkup

Find out what your existing business records reveal about sales, costs, performance, and potential problems, using the spreadsheets you already keep.

What you receive

  • A review of one existing Excel or CSV dataset.
  • Data cleaning and basic quality checks.
  • Analysis of three to five agreed business questions.
  • A concise summary of findings and recommended next steps.
  • A simple Excel report, with a chart or visual summary if useful.

Who this suits

  • Small retailers and distributors tracking sales or inventory.
  • Service businesses tracking revenue and expenses.
  • Small contractors or MEP firms tracking budgets, procurement, and project costs.

How it works

Send the file and the questions you want answered. You get scope, timing, and a fixed fee before any work starts.
